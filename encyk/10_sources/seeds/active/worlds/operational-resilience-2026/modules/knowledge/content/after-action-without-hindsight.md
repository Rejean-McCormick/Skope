# After-Action Review Without Hindsight

> **Simulation note:** Fictional training content for demonstrating Konnaxion workflows. It is not an emergency procedure, SOP, safety directive, or substitute for authorized professional guidance.

Evaluate a decision against the **state of knowledge available when it was made**. Freeze a historical snapshot containing the facts, hypotheses, documents, options, constraints, and unresolved questions visible at that moment. Review later evidence separately. The purpose is learning, not rewriting uncertainty as negligence.
