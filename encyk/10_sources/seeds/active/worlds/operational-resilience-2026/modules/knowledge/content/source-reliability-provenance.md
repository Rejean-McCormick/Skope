# Source Reliability & Provenance

> **Simulation note:** Fictional training content for demonstrating Konnaxion workflows. It is not an emergency procedure, SOP, safety directive, or substitute for authorized professional guidance.

Preserve origin, observation time, report time, scope, confidence, and linked evidence. Two reports can appear contradictory while both are valid for different locations, time windows, or operating conditions. Reconciliation should explain the difference rather than silently replace one record with another.
