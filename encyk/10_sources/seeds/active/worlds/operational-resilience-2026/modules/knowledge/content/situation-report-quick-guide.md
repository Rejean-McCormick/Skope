# Situation Report Quick Guide

> **Simulation note:** Fictional training content for demonstrating Konnaxion workflows. It is not an emergency procedure, SOP, safety directive, or substitute for authorized professional guidance.

A useful situation report states: what changed, what is confirmed, what is uncertain, what decisions are open, what actions are blocked, which capabilities are missing, and which source/evidence revisions support the report. Avoid turning hypotheses into facts.
