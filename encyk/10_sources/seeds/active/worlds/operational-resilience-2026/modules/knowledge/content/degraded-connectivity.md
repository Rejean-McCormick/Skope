# Operating With Degraded Connectivity

> **Simulation note:** Fictional training content for demonstrating Konnaxion workflows. It is not an emergency procedure, SOP, safety directive, or substitute for authorized professional guidance.

Before disconnection, retain the minimum operational snapshot needed for assigned work. During disconnection, continue local logging with stable timestamps and source identity. On reconnect, synchronize append-only local events first, detect conflicts second, and qualify the shared situation explicitly. **Do not use “latest device wins” as a reconciliation rule.**
