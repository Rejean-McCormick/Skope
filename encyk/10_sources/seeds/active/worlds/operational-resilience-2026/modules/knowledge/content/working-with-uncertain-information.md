# Working With Uncertain Information

> **Simulation note:** Fictional training content for demonstrating Konnaxion workflows. It is not an emergency procedure, SOP, safety directive, or substitute for authorized professional guidance.

Keep three things separate: **observation**, **qualification**, and **decision**.

An observation records who saw or reported what and when. Qualification may confirm, narrow, contradict, or supersede an interpretation, but should not erase the original record. A decision records what an authorized person chose using the information available at that time.
