# Dependency Mapping for Operational Work

> **Simulation note:** Fictional training content for demonstrating Konnaxion workflows. It is not an emergency procedure, SOP, safety directive, or substitute for authorized professional guidance.

A task is actionable only when its prerequisites are visible. Model critical resources, timing windows, capacity constraints, authority gates, information requirements, and downstream tasks. When a constraint changes, identify affected actions explicitly instead of assuming every team will recompute the plan independently.
