# Operational Context & Handoff

> **Simulation note:** Fictional training content for demonstrating Konnaxion workflows. It is not an emergency procedure, SOP, safety directive, or substitute for authorized professional guidance.

A handoff transfers **responsibility plus context**, not only a task name.

Minimum handoff:
- current objective;
- what is known and what remains uncertain;
- decisions already made and their basis;
- blocked or dependent actions;
- evidence/document revisions in use;
- next confirmation expected.

A receiver should be able to continue without reconstructing the incident from chat history.
