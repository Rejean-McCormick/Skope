# During the Cyberattack, Six Teams Work in Six Different Realities.

> The technical incident is only half the crisis; the other half is keeping security, operations, leadership, communications and partners synchronized.

## The problem

Security sees indicators of compromise; IT sees broken systems; operations sees unavailable services; legal sees obligations; communications sees public risk; executives need choices. If those streams separate, decisions lag or contradict one another.

## What breaks

- Fragmentation
- Interoperability Failure
- Resource Orchestration Failure
- Context Loss

## Who holds part of the picture

- security
- IT
- operations
- leadership

## With Koali

Koali holds the incident as a governed timeline: facts versus hypotheses, affected services, owners, containment decisions, dependencies, external requests, communications and recovery gates. The post-incident record is produced by the response itself.

## Human authority

Authorized humans and institutions retain their existing legal, professional and democratic authority. Koali structures knowledge, coordination and traceability; it does not take sovereign or professional decisions.

## What success looks like

- The relevant state remains visible across handoffs and time.
- Responsibilities, unresolved questions and escalation paths are explicit.
- The outcome produces reusable memory for the next comparable case.

## Real-world parallels

### Change Healthcare Cybersecurity Incident — U.S. HHS

HHS described the 2024 cyberattack as having unprecedented nationwide impact on patients and health-care providers.

**Relation to scenario:** `DIRECT`  
**Source:** https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/special-topics/change-healthcare-cybersecurity-incident-frequently-asked-questions/index.html

### Turning the Tide Together — Mass Casualty Commission, Volume 5

The Commission documented critical-incident communication, public-warning and inter-agency coordination failures during the 2020 Nova Scotia mass casualty.

**Relation to scenario:** `STRONG ANALOGUE`  
**Source:** https://masscasualtycommission.ca/files/documents/Turning-the-Tide-Together-Volume-5-Policing.pdf

## Boundary

The documented cases illustrate analogous failure mechanisms. They do not establish that Koali would have prevented the event or guaranteed a different outcome.
