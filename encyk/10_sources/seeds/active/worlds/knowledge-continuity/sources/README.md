# Source and provenance note

World 6 — Knowledge Continuity is a **fictional demonstration world**.

All people, organizations, incidents, cases, measurements, decisions and artifacts in this pack are synthetic. They are not claims about real people or organizations.

The pack deliberately separates:

- **Stockage evidence** — synthetic raw artifacts and versioned records;
- **Knowledge** — interpretations and reusable cases built from those artifacts;
- **Konservation** — human/practice provenance and oral-history style narratives;
- **EkoH** — advisory expertise context, with neutral ethics scores;
- **CertifiKation** — demonstrated capability through practice and peer validation;
- **Korum** — case clinics for eliciting reasoning, not consensus manufacture.

No Smart Vote output or public consultation is seeded in v1.
