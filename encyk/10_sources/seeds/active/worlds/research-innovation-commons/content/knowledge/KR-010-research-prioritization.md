# Research Prioritization Is Not Truth Voting

NEXUS uses collective decision tools to allocate scarce research resources, never to declare a hypothesis scientifically true.

## Three readings may coexist
1. **Raw participant reading** — each eligible participant counts equally.
2. **Relevant-expertise reading** — declared EkoH domain relevance changes the advisory reading.
3. **Methods cohort reading** — a prespecified methodological cohort is shown separately.

The raw reading remains visible beside every derived reading.

## What the decision can answer
- Which uncertainty is most consequential?
- Which project is ready to execute?
- Which shared infrastructure unlocks several experiments?
- Which replication should be funded now?

## What it cannot answer
- Which hypothesis is true?
- Which researcher is morally better?
- Which result should be ignored because it is unpopular?

The 2025 historical round is retained as EVID-011 because the different declared lenses produced different orderings. That divergence is information about governance, not evidence about NEX-27.
