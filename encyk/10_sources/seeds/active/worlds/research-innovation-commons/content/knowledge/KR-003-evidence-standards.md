# NEXUS Evidence Standards

## Levels used in this World
1. **Preliminary** — internally coherent finding that has not been independently reproduced.
2. **Supported** — finding survives prespecified checks and has more than one compatible evidence item.
3. **Replicated** — independently reproduced under a declared compatible protocol.
4. **Contested** — credible evidence points in materially different directions.
5. **Contradicted** — a specific prediction failed under conditions that meaningfully test it.

Negative and null results are retained. A failed prediction may weaken a hypothesis without globally falsifying every variant of it.
