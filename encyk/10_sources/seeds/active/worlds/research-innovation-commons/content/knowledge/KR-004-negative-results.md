# Why Negative Results Stay Visible

World 7 treats negative and null findings as first-class research objects.

## A negative result records
- the prediction that was tested;
- the exact protocol and dataset versions;
- the conditions under which the prediction failed;
- the uncertainty and limitations;
- which claims or hypotheses are weakened, contradicted, or left untouched.

## Example
RES-008 is `null`: changing KX-17 did not produce the predicted downstream response under the synthetic conditions. That contradicts a **strong tested causal prediction**, but it does not globally falsify every possible KX-17 mechanism.

## Reuse rule
Before launching a new experiment, search the failure registry for equivalent predictions, conditions, and methods. Repeating a failed approach can still be justified, but the new project must state what changed and why another test is informative.
