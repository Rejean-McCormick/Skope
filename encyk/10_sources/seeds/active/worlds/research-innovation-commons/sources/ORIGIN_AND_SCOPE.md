# World 7 — Origin and scope

This seed develops the user-selected **Research & Innovation Commons** concept around the scenario archetype **Five Countries, One Disease, No Shared Scientific Language** and the supporting arcs **The Experiment That Failed**, **What Do We Do Next?**, and **Promising ≠ Proven**.

All institutions, people, the NEX-27 syndrome, biomarker KX-17, datasets, protocols, experiments and results are deliberately fictional/synthetic. They are demo content for Konnaxion Worlds and must not be interpreted as medical or scientific guidance.

## Canon boundaries
- evidence and provenance remain canonical;
- every canonical claim resolves to evidence, and experimental evidence resolves to result → experiment → protocol version + dataset version → project;
- EkoH represents contextual expertise, not truth or moral worth;
- Smart Vote is used for resource allocation, not scientific truth;
- raw decision readings remain visible beside derived readings;
- negative/null results remain first-class knowledge;
- absence of a seeded Korum stance is not treated as a neutral stance;
- derived summaries never overwrite source objects;
- Konservation is intentionally absent because Knowledge + Stockage already cover the relevant research-memory functions.

## Integration boundary
The ethiKos/EkoH scenario uses the repository's current `ethikos-demo-scenario/v3` contract. Knowledge, CertifiKation, Konstruct, Stockage and Kontact remain explicit module sidecars marked for the future multi-module World bridge; this drop-in does not invent runtime importers that are not present in the supplied Konnaxion snapshot.
