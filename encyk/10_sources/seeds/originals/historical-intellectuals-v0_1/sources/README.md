# Sources and provenance policy

## Taxonomy

UNESCO Institute for Statistics — *International Standard Classification of Education: Fields of Education and Training 2013 (ISCED-F 2013), Detailed Field Descriptions*.

This seed uses ISCED-F only as a domain taxonomy. It does not treat an ISCED-F label as evidence that a person had a modern credential in that field.

## Biographical/source status

The current v0.1 registry is a **canonicalization queue**. Entries are seed candidates assembled for coverage and structure. Before a persona is allowed to produce a published reconstructed proposal, preference or synthetic vote, attach primary sources where available and reliable secondary scholarship, with explicit uncertainty for ancient, legendary, religious or contested identities.
