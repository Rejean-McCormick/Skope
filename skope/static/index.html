What is External Convergence?

  
After the initial traversal, Skope looks for outside entities connected to several entities already in your scope. They may be relevant, but are suggestions, not automatic additions.

  
Selected taxa: 100→Candidate linked to 8 of them→8% support

  
Threshold: minimum percentage of distinct selected entities that support the outside candidate. Minimum connections: minimum number of distinct selected entities, regardless of percentage.

  
Extra depth: how far to traverse after explicitly accepting a candidate; 0 means no further traversal.

  
External convergence is NOT calculated during the massive batch QID inventory. Canada/North America preferences are also NOT used to filter that mass run.
